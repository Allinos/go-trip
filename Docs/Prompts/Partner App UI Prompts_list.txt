Design a modern, scalable Android mobile application UI for a Partner / Driver / Service Provider App for a Taxi & Vehicle Rental platform.

The app should be simple, functional, and distraction-free, optimized for drivers and vehicle owners who need quick actions and clear information.

The UI should visually align with the Passenger App (RideFlow) using a RedBus-inspired red & white theme, while remaining original.

Follow Material Design 3 guidelines and optimize for Expo React Native development.
